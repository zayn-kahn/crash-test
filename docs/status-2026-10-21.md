## Headline findings

1. **The agent model decides whether it gets fooled.** Same 11 attacks, raw key, 44 attack runs per model:

   | Agent | Fooled (money left) | 95% range | Honest payments held back |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | GPT-5 mini | 27/44 (61%) | 47-74% | 2% |
   | Claude Haiku 4.5 | 23/44 (52%) | 38-66% | 1% |
   | Claude Sonnet 4.5 | 23/44 (52%) | 38-66% | 2% |
   | GLM 5.3 Flash | 7/44 (16%) | 8-29% | 2% |
   | DeepSeek V4.1 Flash | 0/44 | 0-8% | 13% |
   | Claude Opus 4.5 | 0/44 | 0-8% | 11% |
   | Claude Haiku 5.5 | 0/264 | 0-1.4% | 3-14% |

   Generation beats price: Haiku 5.5 ($0.003/run) never fell; Sonnet 4.5 (25x the price) fell half the time.
   Within the 4.5 generation only Opus held. The careful models also hold back on ~10% of honest payments.

2. **The wallet decides whether a fooled agent costs money.** Two fooled agents, 4 wallets, no address book,
   22 attack runs per wallet each:

   | Wallet | Leaked (Haiku 4.5 / GPT-5 mini) | Honest payments not made | Owner's tap needed |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | Raw key | 13/22 / 14/22 | 0-1% | none |
   | Policy, caps only | 11/22 / 14/22 | 1-17% | 20-33% |
   | Policy, allowlist | 0/22 / 0/22 | 14-17% | 23-29% |
   | Brake + watcher | 0/22 / 0/22 | 11-13% | none |

   Brake + watcher and the allowlist stopped every fooled payment (0/88 together). Caps alone barely help.
   Wallet-alone ceiling (scripted agent that always falls for it): raw 100%, caps-only 82%, allowlist 0%,
   Brake + watcher 0%.

3. **The owner's tap rubber-stamps.** On caps-only, held payments the owner had asked for (by amount) were
   approved to the attacker's address: 4 (Haiku 4.5) and 3 (GPT-5 mini). Human-in-the-loop on the amount, not
   the address, does not stop a poisoned address.

## Interesting

- An address book in the prompt does not save a weak model: Haiku 4.5 leaked 11/22 with it, 12/22 without;
  GPT-5 mini 13/22 vs 14/22. They had the right address in front of them and paid the planted one.
- "Public requests" mode makes GPT-5 mini much worse (17/22 vs 10/22); Haiku 4.5 a little (13 vs 10).
- The allowlist wallet's prompt paragraph alone makes agents more careful: fooled 27% (GPT-5 mini) and 45%
  (Haiku 4.5) on that wallet vs ~60% elsewhere. It protects twice: prompt and code.
- What fools models: poisoned memory (memory, memory-hard), fake gateway approvals (router, router-hard),
  look-alike sites/addresses (lookalike, spoof-hard). Grok, refund and plain spoof fool almost nobody (only
  GPT-5 mini, rarely).
- Haiku 5.5's defence, read from its runs: check every address against something trusted (contacts, or the
  chain history it reads in 99% of runs), ask the owner on any mismatch.
